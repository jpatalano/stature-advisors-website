Stature Advisors LLC ("Stature Advisors," "we," "us," or "our") operates statureadvisors.com. We provide technology advisory services to founders, business owners, and private equity firms. Our registered address is in the United States.

Questions about this policy may be directed to: privacy@statureadvisors.com

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: When you submit a contact form, we collect your name, email address, company, role, and the content of your message. We do not collect payment information through this website.

Information collected automatically: When you visit our website, we and our service providers may collect standard log data including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and time spent. If you have accepted cookies, we may use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized analytics data about how visitors use the site.

Cookies: See our Cookie Policy for full details on what cookies we use and how to control them.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiry and determine whether an engagement is a good fit

  • Improve our website and understand how visitors engage with it
  • Comply with applicable law and protect our legal rights

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (EEA/UK Visitors)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom, we process your personal data under the following legal bases: (a) your consent, where required; (b) our legitimate interests in operating our business and responding to inquiries; and (c) compliance with legal obligations.

5. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only with:

  • Formspree Inc. — our form processing provider, which routes contact form submissions to our email inbox.
  • Google LLC — analytics services (Google Analytics), if you have accepted cookies. Data is aggregated and anonymized.
  • hCaptcha (Intuition Machines, Inc.) — spam protection on our contact form.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — when required by law or to protect our legal rights.

All third-party providers are bound by their own privacy policies and applicable data protection law.

6. Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for up to 24 months unless a longer retention period is required for a legitimate business purpose (such as an ongoing engagement) or by law. Analytics data is retained per Google Analytics' default retention settings (up to 26 months).

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal obligations.
  • Objection / Restriction: Object to or request restriction of processing in certain circumstances.
  • Data portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (EEA/UK).
  • Opt-out of sale (CCPA): We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is necessary.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@statureadvisors.com. We will respond within 30 days.
